## Bounded Request Contract

Methods `issue(method, body)`, `consume(method, body)` and `reconcile(method, body)` accept explicit slashed form fixtures, never superglobals, and require exact `POST`. All other methods reject without invoking ports. Exact allowed keys and scalar string leaves are checked before JSON work; every leaf is at most 2048 bytes and raw/unslashed encoded bodies at most 4096 bytes. Scalar `stripslashes()` models WordPress form unslashing. Unknown keys (including actor/trusted/site selectors), arrays, missing fields, invalid UTF8 and oversized inputs reject without provider reads.

Canonical unsigned decimal form strings reject signs, spaces, leading zeros except zero, floats, scientific notation, integer/boolean inputs and overflow before casting. Internal counters/times are strict integers, conservatively bounded below PHP_INT_MAX with clock arithmetic headroom. UUID and lower-case digest syntax, exact source/purpose, purpose-specific affirmative `prepare`/`close`, fixed scope, replacement binding and original positive window of at most 300 seconds are validated. No input clock, signature, actor, current capability or serialized projection becomes permission.

Issuance requires current fixture authorization and purpose/site-specific issuance nonce before sensitive context reads. Valid context has exactly site UUID, stored binding, permission generation, eligibility boolean and nullable reviewed replacement binding. Grant requires eligibility; rebind requires a distinct replacement; revoke does not require grant eligibility/old remote pairing. Envelope UUID/clock/epoch/binding are derived from ports, not posted. Authorization/issuance nonce are rechecked before command nonce creation. Issuance mutates no command sink; its envelope/nonce is private ephemeral fixture output, never a support export.

The command nonce action is a fixed-order SHA256 over the entire normalized envelope with the fixture-specific domain. All fields, purpose/confirmation, scope, epoch and deadline are bound independent of posted key order. Only exact core-style verification codes 1/2 are accepted, never truthy strings or booleans. Nonce validity is independent of the application's original 300-second window. Session changes, future issuance, exact expiry, forged context or changing a valid-looking posted field cannot reach the sink.

## Scoped Write Guard and Non-Replaying Observation

Consumption authorizes, verifies nonce and original deadline before calling the sink; it passes the normalized envelope without nonce/session. The sink must invoke the provided short-lived guard before every conditional write attempt. The guard rechecks authorization, nonce/time, fixed valid context, original binding/epoch, eligibility/replacement and authorization/time again after the context read. It permits at most three attempts and never refreshes epoch/deadline/ID. A sink claiming `committed` without a successful last guard, or after a failed/fourth guard, returns unresolved. A retained guard becomes zero-call false in `finally`, including after exceptions, so it is not a later authorization token.

This detects accidental sink miswiring but cannot sandbox malicious callbacks or make capability changes atomic with a CAS. A port could ignore the guard and mutate its own resources; production wiring remains forbidden. Receipt observations deliberately need no fresh write guard: authenticated exact duplicates may be current/superseded even after binding changes, but never write or regrant. The test sink canonicalizes envelope digests, protects command-ID conflicts, advances a fixture epoch once and retains fixed receipt outcomes; it asserts no durable storage or production effect.

Reconciliation is a distinct protected read-intent action bound to fixed site and command ID. It authorizes and verifies its nonce before private lookup, cannot accept posted site/binding/actor, and permits only current/superseded/not-found observations. A consumption nonce cannot authorize a read or vice versa. Original expired commands reject before sink/receipt lookup; a newly authenticated read after expiry can reconcile the old outcome without consuming a refreshed grant. A different currently authorized fixture session may observe because consent is site-owned, not a logged-in session lease.

Only fixed strings from sinks become bounded outcomes; arrays, truthy values, unknown/active/authenticated labels, WP_Error detail and provider exceptions are redacted. Errors are `consent_request_{denied,invalid,context,unresolved}` with a fixed private-model message. Lost acknowledgement may follow a committed fixture write; the result is unresolved until a separately authorized observation, never no-write certainty. Failed/unresolved closure must not claim disabled or resume dependent operations. No adapter response contains raw history, owner, token, SQL, stack/path or permission evidence.
